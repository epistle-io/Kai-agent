KAI TRADING RULES
=================
Never risk more than 1% per trade. Always use stop loss.
Only trade with minimum 1:2 risk to reward ratio.
Wait for candle close confirmation before entering.
Higher timeframe trend must align with trade direction.
Avoid trading during major news events.
London session best for EURUSD and GBPUSD.
New York session best for USD pairs.
RSI above 70 overbought, below 30 oversold.
EMA crossovers confirm momentum.
Never revenge trade after a loss.
Maximum 3 open trades at once.
